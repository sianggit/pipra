## 1. Brand Concept & Positioning

Pipra embodies the convergence of **structured pipeline orchestration** and **aerodynamic avian agility**:
- **Forward Momentum:** Right-facing aerodynamic bird geometry representing rapid, dependable release velocity.
- **Flight Layers:** Folded dual-wing structure evoking multi-stage deployment phases and atomic cutover.
- **Illuminated Spectrum:** Electric cyan, royal blue, and vivid violet gradients symbolizing live operational telemetry, observability, and precision release engineering.

## 3. Official Color Palette & Swatches

### 3.1 Accent Gradients (Flight Feathers)
- **Top Wing Gradient (Sky to Royal Blue):**
  - Start: `#38bdf8` (Electric Sky Cyan)
  - Mid: `#2563eb` (Royal Blue)
  - End: `#1e40af` (Deep Blue)
- **Bottom Wing Gradient (Indigo to Violet/Purple):**
  - Start: `#3b82f6` (Vivid Blue)
  - Mid: `#6366f1` (Electric Indigo)
  - End: `#c084fc` (Rich Purple/Violet)
- **Head & Chest Silhouette (Forward Edge):**
  - Gradient from pure white `#ffffff` through `#f8fafc` down to metallic slate `#94a3b8`.
  - Focal eye circle: `#080c14` (Midnight Black).

## 4. Clear Space & Minimum Sizing Rules

- **Clear Space:** Minimum clear space surrounding the mark must equal the width of the head element (`X` margin on all 4 quadrants).
- **Minimum Sizing:**
  - Standalone symbol: Minimum width **24px** (maintains full legibility of wing layers and eye).
  - Primary horizontal lockup: Minimum width **120px**.
- **Favicon Specification:** Rendered on a rounded squircle (radius 25%) in deep midnight navy `#090d16` with a subtle 1px border.
